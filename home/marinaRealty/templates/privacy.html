Privacy Policy

How Marina Realty collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

Last Updated

1 January 2026

Marina Realty ("we", "us", or "our") operates www.marinarealty.in and is committed to protecting the privacy of our clients, visitors, and website users. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your personal data, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India).

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide to us. When you submit our contact form, enquire about a property, or reach out by phone or email, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, the service you are interested in, and the details of your enquiry.

Information collected automatically. When you visit this website, we may collect technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, and the date and time of your visit. This helps us understand how the site is used and keep it secure.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and schedule appointments or site visits;
  • Provide property listings and real estate services you have requested;
  • Send you updates about properties that match your stated interest, where you have consented;
  • Improve and protect our website, and prevent fraudulent or unauthorised activity;
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We only share it with:

  • Property owners and service providers involved in a transaction you have enquired about;
  • Trusted vendors who help us operate the website or send communications, under confidentiality agreements;
  • Authorities where required by law, regulation, or legal process.

4. Data Retention

We keep your personal information only as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy or as required by law. When it is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it securely.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access the personal information we hold about you, request corrections, withdraw consent, and ask us to delete your data. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

We will respond to your request within a reasonable time and, where applicable, as required by law.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information from loss, misuse, and unauthorised access. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to keep your data safe.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for individuals aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a minor has provided us with their data, please contact us and we will remove it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Your continued use of the website after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
