Our Story

Built on trust, one closing at a time.

Meridian Properties opened its doors in 2011 with two agents and a rented desk above a coffee shop. We had no big-brand backing — just a belief that clients deserve a straight answer, even when it's not the one they want to hear.

That approach is still how we operate today. Every listing is reviewed in person, every offer is explained line by line, and every client — first-time renter or repeat investor — gets the same level of attention.

Fifteen years on, we've helped families, landlords, and first-time buyers move through more than 500 transactions across the city, and we're just getting started.
